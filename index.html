ARES 23247 — West Virginia Robotics Team in Morgantown

ARES 23247 is a West Virginia robotics team and FIRST® Tech Challenge program based in Morgantown, West Virginia. We build competition robots, develop technical leaders, and expand hands-on STEM opportunities for students and our community.

ARES Analytics

ARES Analytics is one of the team's open-source robotics software projects. Its local tools work without Google; optional Google Drive sync accesses only the workspace destination a user selects.
